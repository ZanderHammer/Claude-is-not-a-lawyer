# Imprint (Impressum / legal notice / mentions légales / aviso legal)

Checklist items point to sources. **Fetch and read every provision before you rely on it.**
Imprint data must be exactly as registered: copy names, register numbers and VAT IDs from the
records, which come from the user, never from guesses.

## When is it required

- **EU baseline**: every information society service provider must give general information
  (EU-ECD Art. 5). Member states implement it, often strictly:
  - **Germany**: DE-DDG § 5 for "geschäftsmäßige, in der Regel gegen Entgelt angebotene digitale
    Dienste" (in practice almost any commercial site, incl. ad-financed blogs); DE-MSTV § 18 for
    all non-private telemedia, with extra duties for journalistic-editorial content.
  - **Austria**: AT-ECG § 5 + AT-UGB § 14 (or GewO § 63) + AT-MEDIENG § 25 (disclosure).
  - **France**: LCEN "mentions légales" (MS-FR); **Spain**: LSSI art. 10 (MS-ES).
- **UK**: UK-ECR2002 reg. 6 + company disclosures UK-TRADING-DISCLOSURES regs. 24–25.
- **Switzerland**: CH-UWG Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. s for e-commerce (identity and contact incl. e-mail).
- **US**: no general imprint duty. Use a contact page; DMCA agent details go in the terms.
- **DSA**: points of contact for authorities and users (EU-DSA Art. 11–12) are often published
  next to the imprint.

## Checklist: Germany (DE-DDG § 5, DE-MSTV § 18)

| Item | Source |
|---|---|
| Name and address where established (no P.O. box); legal persons: legal form, authorised representatives; capital if stated | DE-DDG § 5(1) Nr. 1 |
| Fast electronic contact and direct communication, incl. e-mail | DE-DDG § 5(1) Nr. 2 |
| Second direct channel besides e-mail (phone or contact form): established practice based on case law that is **not bundled**; include it and label the source accordingly in the defense report | (case law, not bundled) |
| Supervisory authority for activities requiring official approval | DE-DDG § 5(1) Nr. 3 |
| Register (Handels-, Vereins-, Partnerschafts-, Genossenschaftsregister) and number | DE-DDG § 5(1) Nr. 4 |
| Regulated professions: chamber, statutory title and state where awarded, professional rules and how to access them | DE-DDG § 5(1) Nr. 5 |
| USt-IdNr. / Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer, if available | DE-DDG § 5(1) Nr. 6 |
| Liquidation status, if applicable | DE-DDG § 5(1) Nr. 7 |
| Journalistic-editorial content: responsible person with name and address | DE-MSTV § 18(2) |
| Consumer dispute resolution statement (if ≥ 11 employees or committed) | DE-VSBG § 36 |
| "Easily recognisable, directly accessible and permanently available" (link label "Impressum", max. two clicks is common practice) | DE-DDG § 5(1) (wording) |


## Checklist: Austria

| Item | Source |
|---|---|
| Name/company, geographic address, fast contact incl. e-mail, Firmenbuch number and court, supervisory authority, chamber/professional rules, VAT ID | AT-ECG § 5(1) Z 1–7 |
| Company name, legal form, seat, Firmenbuch number and court (registered companies) | AT-UGB § 14 |
| Disclosure (Offenlegung): media owner etc.; reduced for "small" websites | AT-MEDIENG § 25 (esp. § 25(5)) |
| Consumer ADR information (if obliged/committed) | AT-ASTG § 19 |
| Practical model imprints | AT-WKO-IMPRESSUM |

## Checklist: UK

| Item | Source |
|---|---|
| Name, geographic address, e-mail, trade/company register and number, supervisory authority, professional body, VAT number | UK-ECR2002 reg. 6 |
| Companies: registered name, registered number, part of the UK where registered, registered office | UK-TRADING-DISCLOSURES regs. 24–25 |

## Checklist: Switzerland

| Item | Source |
|---|---|
| Clear and complete identity and contact address incl. e-mail | CH-UWG Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. s Ziff. 1 |
| Commercial register entry and UID (good practice; check the OR / commercial register rules) | CH-OR (lawyer question if needed) |

## Pitfalls

- Using a P.O. box or a virtual office the user does not actually use (DE/AT require the address
  where the business is established).
- Missing a second contact channel in Germany (see above).
- Inventing register numbers or VAT IDs ("[[TODO]]" instead!).
- Still linking the EU ODR platform (repealed, EU-ODR-REPEAL).
- Sole traders' home addresses: they must often be published; warn the user and record it.
