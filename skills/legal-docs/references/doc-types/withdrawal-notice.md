# Withdrawal notice (Widerrufsbelehrung / Rücktrittsbelehrung / cancellation information)

Checklist items point to sources. **Fetch and read every provision before you rely on it.**
This is the one document where you **copy statutory model text verbatim** and fill in the
blanks according to the official design notes. Never paraphrase the model. A correct use of the
model gives legal certainty, a creative version removes it and can extend the withdrawal period
(DE: up to 12 months and 14 days, DE-BGB-WIDERRUF § 356(3)–(4)).

## When is it required

Distance or off-premises contracts between a trader and a **consumer** in the EU/EEA (EU-CRD
Art. 9 ff.) or UK (UK-CCR2013 regs. 29 ff.), unless an exception applies (EU-CRD Art. 16;
UK-CCR2013 reg. 28; DE-BGB-FERNABSATZ § 312g(2); AT-FAGG § 18). Not required for pure B2B.
Switzerland has no general online withdrawal right (CH-OR). US: no general right (some state
rules for specific sales).

## Deliverables

1. Withdrawal notice (`documents/withdrawal-notice.<lang>.md`), based on the official model in
   the official language version
2. Model withdrawal form (`documents/withdrawal-form.<lang>.md`)
3. Implementation notes: electronic withdrawal function (from 19 June 2026), confirmation e-mail
   on a durable medium, consent + acknowledgement checkbox for early start of digital content /
   services, order button

## Pitfalls

- Translating the German model yourself instead of using the official language version of the
  model for each country.
- Mixing the options of the model's design notes (goods vs. services, single vs. multiple
  deliveries) incorrectly.
- Forgetting the consent + acknowledgement checkbox for early start, which is needed for the
  right to expire.
- Putting the withdrawal notice only inside the terms without making it available on a durable
  medium (order confirmation e-mail).
