# United Kingdom: sources

UK legislation is published by The National Archives on **legislation.gov.uk** ("latest available
(revised)" view by default; watch the "Changes to legislation" banner). After Brexit the GDPR
continues as the **UK GDPR**, a separate, amended text. Never cite the EU version for UK
obligations, or the other way round. The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA) amended the UK GDPR,
DPA 2018 and PECR; its provisions commenced in stages through 2026.

Pinpoints: `/article/13`, `/section/62`, `/regulation/6`, `/schedule/3`.

## Data protection and privacy

### UK-GDPR — UK General Data Protection Regulation (retained Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as amended)
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: General Data Protection Regulation
- Also: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2016/679/article/13
- Key provisions: Art. 3 (territorial scope); Art. 8 (age of consent for information society services offered directly to a child: 13); Art. 6 (lawful bases, incl. "recognised legitimate interests" added by the DUAA, check the current text); Art. 13/14 (privacy notice content); Art. 15–22 (rights); Art. 27 (UK representative for non-UK controllers); Art. 28 (processors); Art. 44 ff. (international transfers, amended by the DUAA)
- Used for: privacy-policy, dpa
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### UK-DPA2018 — Data Protection Act 2018
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Data Protection Act 2018
- Key provisions: Sch. 1 (conditions for special category data); Part 5 (ICO)
- Used for: privacy-policy
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### UK-PECR — Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Privacy and Electronic Communications
- Also: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/6 https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/schedule/A1 https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/22
- Key provisions: reg. 6 (storing or accessing information on terminal equipment, "subject to Schedule A1"); **Sch. A1 (exceptions from consent inserted by the DUAA, e.g. strictly necessary, and conditional exceptions such as statistical purposes and appearance/functionality; read the conditions, incl. the right to object)**; reg. 22 (e-mail marketing to individuals, soft opt-in in reg. 22(3))
- Used for: cookie-policy, privacy-policy (newsletter)
- Notes: Amended 5 Feb 2026 per the legislation.gov.uk timeline. Advertising/tracking cookies still need consent.
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### UK-DUAA — Data (Use and Access) Act 2025
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/18/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Data (Use and Access) Act 2025
- Key provisions: Part 5 (amendments to UK GDPR / DPA 2018, e.g. recognised legitimate interests, automated decision-making, complaints handling: controllers must facilitate complaints); s. 112 + Sch. 12 (cookies, PECR Sch. A1); Sch. 13 (PECR enforcement, higher fines)
- Used for: privacy-policy, cookie-policy
- Notes: Check commencement status per provision ("Changes to legislation" / commencement regulations). Data protection provisions were reported fully commenced by June 2026.
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### UK-ICO-CHILDREN — ICO: Age appropriate design code (Children's code)

### UK-ICO-FEE — ICO: Data protection fee
- URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/

## Consumer, contract, e-commerce

### UK-CRA2015 — Consumer Rights Act 2015
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Consumer Rights Act 2015
- Also: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/65
- Key provisions: Part 1 (goods, digital content, services: statutory rights that cannot be excluded, e.g. ss. 31, 47, 57); Part 2 (unfair terms: s. 62 fairness test, s. 64 core terms transparency, s. 65 no exclusion of liability for death/personal injury from negligence, s. 68 transparency, Sch. 2 grey list)
- Used for: terms (B2C)
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### UK-CCR2013 — Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013
- Also: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/schedule/3 https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/29 https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/36
- Key provisions: Sch. 2 (information for distance contracts); reg. 14 (order button "order with obligation to pay"); regs. 29–31 (14-day cancellation); reg. 28 (contracts excluded from cancellation); reg. 36–37 (services and digital content started during the cancellation period: express request / consent and acknowledgement); Sch. 3 (model cancellation form and instructions)
- Used for: terms, withdrawal-notice (UK "cancellation")
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### UK-DMCC — Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/13/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024
- Key provisions: Part 4 Ch. 1 (unfair commercial practices, in force since April 2025, incl. drip pricing and fake reviews, Sch. 20); Part 4 Ch. 2 (subscription contracts: pre-contract information, reminder notices, easy exit, cooling-off)
- Used for: terms (subscriptions, pricing)
- Notes: The **subscription contracts regime was not yet in force** at last verification (government announced commencement for 2027). Check commencement before drafting subscription clauses, and design for it anyway.
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### UK-OSA — Online Safety Act 2023
- URL: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/50/contents
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Online Safety Act 2023
- Key provisions: Duties for user-to-user and search services, incl. clear and accessible terms of service on how users are protected from illegal content (and children's protection where applicable), complaints procedures
- Used for: terms (services with user-generated content and UK users)
- Notes: Ofcom codes of practice give the detail (T2). Check what applies to the service's size and type.
- Last verified: 2026-10-17
