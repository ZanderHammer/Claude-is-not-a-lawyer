# Austria: sources

Austrian federal law is published in the **Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes (RIS)**.
`GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=<n>` shows the whole law in its current
version ("Fassung vom <today>"). Austria implements the EU directives listed in `eu.md`, and the
GDPR applies directly. The DSG adds national rules.

Imprint duties in Austria come from **three** laws at once: ECG § 5, UGB § 14 (or GewO § 63) and
MedienG § 25 (Offenlegung). A webshop needs all three.

## Imprint and disclosure

### AT-ECG — E-Commerce-Gesetz (§ 5 Allgemeine Informationen; §§ 9–11 contracts)
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20001703
- Tier: T1
- Expect: E-Commerce-Gesetz
- Key provisions: § 5(1) Z 1–7 (name/company; geographic address; fast direct contact incl. e-mail; Firmenbuch number and court; supervisory authority; chamber/professional association, title and applicable professional rules; VAT ID); § 5(2) (prices incl. taxes and shipping); § 9 (information before contract conclusion); § 10 (order confirmation, correction of input errors); § 11 (terms must be storable and reproducible)
- Used for: imprint, terms
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-UGB — Unternehmensgesetzbuch § 14 (information on business letters and websites)
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10001702
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Unternehmensgesetzbuch
- Key provisions: § 14 (registered companies: company name, legal form, seat, Firmenbuch number and court, on websites too)
- Used for: imprint (companies in the Firmenbuch). Non-registered trade businesses: GewO § 63
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-MEDIENG — Mediengesetz § 25 (Offenlegung) and § 24 (Impressum)
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10000719
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Mediengesetz
- Key provisions: § 25(1)–(4) (disclosure for periodic media incl. websites: media owner, ownership structure, "grundlegende Richtung"); § 25(5) (reduced disclosure for "small" websites without content beyond presenting the owner that could influence public opinion: name/company, Unternehmensgegenstand if any, residence/seat of the media owner)
- Used for: imprint
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-WKO-IMPRESSUM — WKO: "Das korrekte Website-Impressum" (guidance with model imprints)
- URL: https://www.wko.at/oe/internetrecht/das-korrekte-website-impressum.pdf
- Tier: T2
- Key provisions: Explains how ECG, UGB, GewO and MedienG combine, with model imprints per legal form (separate PDFs for GmbH, OG, KG, e.U., Verein, EU company)
- Used for: imprint
- Notes: WKO is the statutory Austrian Economic Chamber (a public-law body). Its guidance is not law; check against the statutes above.
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

## Contracts and consumer law

### AT-FAGG — Fern- und Auswärtsgeschäfte-Gesetz
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20008847
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Fern- und Auswärtsgeschäfte-Gesetz
- Key provisions: § 4 (pre-contractual information); § 8 (order button); § 11 (14-day withdrawal right, "Rücktritt"); **§ 13a (Rücktrittsfunktion: electronic withdrawal function for contracts concluded via an online interface)**; § 18 (exceptions, incl. digital content after consent); Anhang I (model withdrawal instructions and form)
- Used for: terms, withdrawal-notice
- Notes: § 13a and the updated Anhang I were inserted by BGBl. I Nr. 59/2026 (implementing `EU-DIR-2023-2673`). Read the in-force provisions at the end of the FAGG for the start date.
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-KSCHG — Konsumentenschutzgesetz
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10002462
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Konsumentenschutzgesetz
- Key provisions: § 6 (terms that are void in consumer contracts, incl. § 6(3) transparency); § 9 (warranty cannot be excluded); § 14 (jurisdiction for consumers)
- Used for: terms (B2C)
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-ASTG — Alternative-Streitbeilegung-Gesetz
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20009242
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Alternative-Streitbeilegung-Gesetz
- Key provisions: § 19 (information on consumer ADR bodies on the website/terms if the trader is obliged or has committed to use them)
- Used for: imprint, terms
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-BAFG — Barrierefreiheitsgesetz (implements the EAA)
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20012316
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Barrierefreiheitsgesetz
- Key provisions: Scope incl. consumer e-commerce services; exemption for micro-enterprises providing services; information on accessibility of the service (in the terms or otherwise clearly perceivable)
- Used for: terms / accessibility statement (since 28 June 2025)
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

## Data protection

### AT-DSG — Datenschutzgesetz
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10001597
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Datenschutzgesetz
- Key provisions: § 1 (constitutional right to data protection); § 4(4) (age of consent for information society services offered directly to a child: 14); §§ 12–13 (image processing / video surveillance); § 24 (complaint to the Datenschutzbehörde)
- Used for: privacy-policy
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-TKG-165 — Telekommunikationsgesetz 2021 § 165(3) (cookies, terminal equipment)
- URL: https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20011678
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Telekommunikationsgesetz 2021
- Key provisions: § 165(3) (providers of information society services must inform users; storing/accessing data on the device only with active consent based on clear and comprehensive information, unless solely for transmission or strictly necessary for an explicitly requested service); § 174 (unsolicited messages / e-mail marketing)
- Used for: cookie-policy, privacy-policy (newsletter)
- Last verified: 2026-10-17

### AT-DSB — Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde
- URL: https://www.dsb.gv.at/
- Tier: T2
- Bot-protected: yes
- Key provisions: Supervisory authority (contact details for the complaint clause); guidance and decisions
- Used for: privacy-policy
- Notes: The site refused automated connections at last verification. Open in a browser or use `EU-EDPB-MEMBERS` for the contact details.
- Last verified: 2026-10-17
