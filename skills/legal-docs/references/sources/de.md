# Germany: sources

German federal law is published by the Federal Ministry of Justice on
**gesetze-im-internet.de** (non-binding but official consolidated text; the binding text is the
Bundesgesetzblatt). One page per section: `https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/<law>/__<n>.html`.
State treaties (Medienstaatsvertrag) are published by the states, e.g. on gesetze-bayern.de.

Supervisory authorities: the BfDI is federal; private companies are supervised by the **state**
DPA of their seat (list: `EU-EDPB-MEMBERS` or `DE-DSK`). Use the state DPA in the complaint clause.

- URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html
- Tier: T1
- Expect: Allgemeine Informationspflichten
- Key provisions: § 5(1) Nr. 1 (name, address; legal form, representatives, capital if stated), Nr. 2 (fast electronic contact incl. e-mail), Nr. 3 (supervisory authority for licensed activities), Nr. 4 (register and number), Nr. 5 (regulated professions: chamber, title, professional rules), Nr. 6 (USt-IdNr./W-IdNr.), Nr. 7 (liquidation)

- Last verified: 2026-10-17
