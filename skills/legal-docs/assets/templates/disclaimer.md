# Disclaimer texts

Use these verbatim (translate them if the user works in another language, keeping the meaning).

## Long form

Use it at the start of the session, in every defense report, in `docs/legal/README.md`, and in
the final hand-off message.

> **I am not a lawyer, and this is not legal advice.**
> I am an AI coding agent. I can draft legal documents based on the official sources bundled with
> this skill, your answers and your code, and I explain every choice I make. But I can
> misread the law, miss a rule, apply the wrong rule to your situation, or rely on facts that are
> incomplete. Laws and court decisions change, and they differ between countries.
> Everything I produce is a **draft for review by a qualified lawyer** in the relevant
> jurisdiction(s). Do not publish or sign any of it before that review. You remain responsible for
> the documents you use.

## Short form

Use it at every phase hand-off and whenever you state what a law requires.

> Reminder: I'm not a lawyer. This is a draft based on the sources cited; have it checked by a
> qualified lawyer before use.

## Banner for generated documents

Put it at the top of every document in `docs/legal/documents/`.

```markdown
> **DRAFT – generated by an AI coding agent, not legal advice, not reviewed by a lawyer.**
> Do not publish before a qualified lawyer has reviewed it. Defense report:
> `docs/legal/defense/<doc>.md`. Version <0.x>, <YYYY-MM-DD>.
```
